### About

I'm Mello, an independent software developer and business owner based in Brazil. I turn ideas into useful software, with an emphasis on clarity, reliability and good design.

My interests lie in **web platforms, developer tools, intelligent systems and automation**. I enjoy solving practical problems and building interfaces that feel natural to use.
